ABOUT THE MUSEUM

The museum is dedicated to preserving and sharing Latin American colonial art, with a special focus on representing the cultural and social diversity of the colonial era.

Our mission is to offer a critical, contemporary perspective on colonial artistic heritage, highlighting the historical narratives that have been overlooked or oversimplified over time.
